Roasted Tomato & Red Pepper Soup
The first cold evening of the year calls for this soup. Roasting the vegetables first gives it a deep, almost smoky sweetness that you just don't get from simmering alone.

Prep time: 15 minutes
Total time: 1 hour 10 minutes
Makes 6 bowls

Ingredients
1.5 kg ripe plum tomatoes, halved
2 red bell peppers, seeded and cut into strips
1 large red onion, cut into wedges
6 cloves garlic, unpeeled
3 tbsp olive oil
1 tsp smoked paprika
4 cups vegetable stock
Salt and pepper
Crusty bread, for serving
To garnish: fresh basil leaves

Method
1. Heat the oven to 220°C. Spread the tomatoes, peppers, onion and garlic on a large tray, drizzle with the olive oil and sprinkle with the paprika, salt and pepper.
2. Roast for 40 minutes, until the edges are charred and the tomatoes have collapsed.
3. Squeeze the garlic out of its skins and tip everything into a large pot with the stock.
4. Simmer for 10 minutes, then blend until smooth.
5. Stir in the cream and warm through without letting it boil.
6. Ladle into bowls and top with the basil.

Tip: the soup thickens in the fridge, so when reheating it the next day
add a splash of water or stock until it loosens up again.
