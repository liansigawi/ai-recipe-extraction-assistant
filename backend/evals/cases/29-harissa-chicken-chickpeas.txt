One-Pan Harissa Chicken with Chickpeas
This has been on repeat at our place since the clocks went back. Everything goes in one pan, and the chickpeas soak up all the juices.

Feeds 6
Prep: 15 min
Cook: 50 min

For the chicken:
8 bone-in chicken thighs, skin on
2 tbsp harissa paste
3 tbsp olive oil
1 tsp ground cumin
2 cans chickpeas, drained
1 red onion, cut into wedges
a handful of cherry tomatoes
Salt and pepper

For the herb salsa:
1 bunch parsley, chopped
1/2 bunch mint, chopped
Zest and juice of 1 lemon
4 tbsp olive oil

Method:
Heat the oven to 200°C.
Mix the harissa, olive oil and cumin, and rub it all over the chicken thighs.
Spread the chickpeas, onion and tomatoes in a large roasting pan and season with salt and pepper.
Lay the chicken on top, skin side up, and roast for 45-50 minutes until the skin is crisp.
While it roasts, stir together all the salsa ingredients with a pinch of salt.
Spoon the salsa over the chicken just before serving.

Tips:
If the skin isn't crisp enough, slide the pan under the grill for the last 3 minutes.
You can swap the chickpeas for white beans.
