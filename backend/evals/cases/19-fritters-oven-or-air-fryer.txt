Crispy Chickpea & Zucchini Fritters
We're on a no-frying kick this month, so I tested these two ways and honestly both are winners.
Recipe inspired by @greenplate.daily 🌱
Makes about 14 fritters | Prep 15 min

Ingredients:
1 (15 oz) can chickpeas, drained and patted dry
2 medium zucchini, coarsely grated and squeezed
1/3 cup crumbled feta
2 green onions, thinly sliced
1 clove garlic, minced
2-3 tbsp chickpea flour
1 tsp ground cumin
a pinch of chili flakes
Salt and black pepper
Olive oil spray
Lemon wedges, for serving

For the yogurt sauce:
1/2 cup Greek yogurt
1 tbsp lemon juice
a handful of fresh dill, chopped

Mash the chickpeas with a fork, leaving some texture.
Add the zucchini, feta, green onions, garlic, flour, cumin, chili flakes, salt and pepper and mix well.
Shape into 14 small patties.
Stir the yogurt, lemon juice and dill together and keep in the fridge until serving.

Oven:
Heat the oven to 220°C and line a tray with baking paper.
Arrange the fritters, spray with olive oil and bake for 25 minutes, flipping halfway.

Air fryer:
Preheat the air fryer to 200°C.
Spray the basket and the fritters with olive oil and cook in batches for 12 minutes, flipping after 8 minutes.

Tip: if you don't have chickpea flour, regular flour or breadcrumbs work just as well.
