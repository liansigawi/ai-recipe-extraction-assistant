Chickpea & Feta Lunch Salad
Serves 2 | Ready in 10 minutes

1 (15 oz) can chickpeas, drained and rinsed
1 cucumber, diced
1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
1/3 cup crumbled feta
2–3 tbsp olive oil
Juice of 1 lemon
Salt and pepper, to taste
A handful of fresh parsley, chopped

Toss everything together in a large bowl. Season to taste and serve right away, or chill for up to 2 hours.
